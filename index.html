About Me

I work at the intersection of climate data and financial protection for smallholder farmers — designing index-based and indemnity-based insurance systems, and the platforms that run them. That means pulling satellite imagery, weather station records and field-collected data into one pipeline, then turning it into claims farmers can actually receive.

Over the past three years I've led multi-district field implementations in Nepal, contributed to securing more than NPR 1 crore in incubation and project funding, and spent a lot of time in training rooms teaching agents, technicians and farmers how to use the tools we build.
